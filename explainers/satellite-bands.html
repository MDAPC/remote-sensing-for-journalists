Seeing in colors you can't

Every surface reflects light in its own pattern, like a fingerprint. Satellites measure that pattern in slices called bands. Choose the right slices and hidden things appear: burn scars under smoke, stressed crops, flooded fields.

Light fingerprints

Each line shows how much light a surface reflects at each wavelength. The columns are the 13 bands of Sentinel-2, the free satellite you can use in Copernicus Browser. Our eyes only see the narrow rainbow on the left. Click any column to learn what it's good for.

What to remember

Bands are ingredients

Each band is one measurement of reflected light. Indices like NDVI or NBR are recipes that combine bands to highlight one thing.

False color is honest

Infrared is invisible, so we paint it with visible colors. Always tell readers which bands an image uses.

Longer waves see further

Shortwave infrared passes through smoke and haze that block visible light. That's why burn scars appear in SWIR.
